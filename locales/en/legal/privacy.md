# Privacy policy

## 1. What is processed

- Telegram id, name and username — for sign-in and support.
- Email — only if you provided one for receipts.
- Provider payment events: payment id, amount, status. Card details never reach
  the store and are not stored.
- Technical records: request path, time and request id.

## 2. Why

The data is needed to issue and renew a subscription, accept payment, produce
receipts and answer support requests.

## 3. Retention

Financial records are kept as long as accounting and reporting require. Other
personal data is deleted or anonymized on request.

## 4. Who receives it

Only the payment and fiscal providers chosen by the store owner, and only to the
extent needed to process a payment. Data is never sold.

## 5. Your rights

You may request access to your data, its correction, or anonymization of your
account. Send requests to support: {support}. Anonymization removes personal
identifiers and keeps only the financial records that must be retained.

## 6. Cookies

The site {domain} uses functional cookies for the sign-in session, the selected
language and the referral link. No advertising cookies are used.

## 7. Contact

The data controller is the owner of {brand}. Contact: {support}.
