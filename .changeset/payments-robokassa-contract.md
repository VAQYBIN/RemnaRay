Bring Robokassa to its documented contract (section 11.3.4). `InvId` is now the invoice's `numeric_id`, `Shp_inv` carries the invoice id, ResultURL is verified as `MD5(OutSum:InvId:Password#2:Shp_*)` at `/webhooks/robokassa/result` and answered `OK<InvId>` from the notification itself, receipts are signed URL-encoded, status is polled through `OpStateExt`, and test mode uses `IsTest=1` with the test passwords. Previously every real ResultURL failed its signature check and the payment link carried a non-numeric `InvId`.

⚠ Configuration: the keys are now `merchantLogin`, `password1`, `password2`, `isTest`, `testPassword1`, `testPassword2`, `sno`, `tax` (`test` and `algorithm` are gone); select MD5 and set ResultURL to `https://<domain>/webhooks/robokassa/result` in the Robokassa settings.
