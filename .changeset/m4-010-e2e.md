Add the Playwright end-to-end suite for the site, the account and the
administration console, and run it in CI. The suite exercises a real stack —
PostgreSQL 18 and Valkey 9.1 in Testcontainers, the API and the site from their
production builds behind one reverse proxy — and fixes what it found: the site
rejected its own versioned theme assets under Next.js 16, `GET /api/v1/me` was
declared twice, `RbacGuard` was not exported by the administration module, and
the `/auth/tg` and `/r/<code>` redirects leaked the internal host.
