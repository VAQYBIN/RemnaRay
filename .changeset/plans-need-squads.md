Never sell a plan that grants no access. The panel receives a plan's squads as the customer's squads, and the console created every plan without any, so a buyer lost all squads. A plan now needs at least one squad in the API, the wizard and the database (section 8), and the console edits every plan field, with the squads loaded from the panel.
